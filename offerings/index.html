Can each person order individually?

That may be an option for suitable print-on-demand products. Other items work better as a group order. We'll check the product, minimum quantity and shipping options before recommending a route.

What will it cost, and when will it arrive?

Pricing depends on the item, artwork, quantity and delivery needs. We'll agree on any design or coordination fee before work begins, then confirm product costs, shipping and production timing with the selected supplier before you place an order.

Do we need a finished design?

No. You can bring an existing logo you have permission to use, or describe an idea. We'll agree on the design work and revisions, and you'll review the artwork before anything goes to print.
